🎓 The Jevons Paradox

What is it?

The Jevons Paradox states that as technology makes resource use more efficient, total consumption of that resource may actually INCREASE instead of decrease.

In Agriculture:

  • Better farming techniques = more crops per acre
  • Higher productivity = more profit per acre
  • More profit = incentive to farm MORE land
  • Result: Total land use INCREASES!

Your Goal:

Watch what happens as you upgrade your farming technology. Even though you COULD produce the same amount on less land, will you?

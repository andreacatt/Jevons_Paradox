🎓 The Jevons Paradox

What is it?

As technology makes resource use more efficient, total consumption may INCREASE instead of decrease.

In This Game:

  • 🔬 Upgrade tech → more crops per plot
  • 💰 Higher productivity → more profit
  • 🌲 More profit → incentive to clear forests
  • 📈 Result: Land use INCREASES!

Your Challenge:

Compete for 20 turns. You and the computer both want to maximize profits. Watch how efficiency drives expansion!
